🎮 1. What is Brainrot GO?

Brainrot GO is a real-world geolocation exploration game built on Leaflet maps. Players walk around their actual neighborhoods to discover, battle, and catch wild internet memes ("Rots"). Build your ultimate team, conquer gyms, battle AI or online PVP players, and climb the ranks!

🪙 2. The $ROT Coin & Token-Gated Battle Pass

The entire ecosystem is powered by the $ROT token on Solana. To prevent bots and reward true community members, the elite 100-Level Battle Pass is strictly token-gated.

🔒 Token Gate Requirement: You must hold at least 100,000 $ROT in your connected Solana wallet to unlock the Battle Pass tracker, level up via catches, and claim high-tier rewards all the way up to the legendary Level 100 OG Godfather Rot!

⚡ 3. How to Set Up a Phantom Wallet

To hold $ROT and unlock the Battle Pass, you need a decentralized Web3 wallet like Phantom:

  • Step 1: Go to phantom.app or download the Phantom app on your iOS/Android device or browser extension (Chrome/Brave).
  • Step 2: Create a new wallet. Make sure to write down your Secret Recovery Phrase and store it in a safe place. Never share it with anyone!
  • Step 3: Fund your wallet with a little bit of Solana (SOL) to pay for gas fees when swapping tokens.
