👻 GHOST HUNTER GO: FIELD GUIDE

Track, Trace, and Trap. Your complete manual to anomalies, weapons, the $GHOST token, and the Hunter Pass.

🌍 1. What is Ghost Hunter Go?

Ghost Hunter Go is a real-world geolocation exploration game built on Leaflet maps. Players walk around their actual neighborhoods to discover, battle, and capture wild paranormal entities. Build your ultimate squad, conquer local Gyms, duel online PVP players, and climb the hunter ranks!

⚔️ 2. First-Person Combat & The Vault

When you spot an anomaly on the map, click it to enter first-person combat.

  • Strike: Use the STRIKE button to swing your equipped weapon. Damage scales based on your weapon's Strength (ATK).
  • Survive: After you strike, the entity will counter-attack. Keep an eye on your Hunter Vitality (HP). If it hits 0, you die and flee the battle.
  • Capture: Reduce the anomaly's HP to 0 to bind it. It is instantly purified and sent to your Vault, where you can view its stats, rarity, and level.

🛡️ 3. The Armory (Weapons & Gear)

Your bare fists won't do much against high-level cryptids. You must equip relics and weapons to survive.

  • Open the Menu -> Inventory & Gear, or click your Avatar in the top right to open your Hunter Profile.
  • Scroll down to your Armory Collection to view the weapons you own.
  • Click EQUIP to place a weapon in your Hunter's hand. This visually updates your avatar and massively boosts your ATK stat in combat.
